How LexDesk Works

AI-Native. Desktop-First.
Engineered for Security.

LexDesk sits on every one of your employees' desktops and connects to AI models through a stateless API key — giving your team intelligent, workflow-specific tools without ever storing your firm's data in the cloud.

🔑

Stateless API Key — No Data Retention

LexDesk connects to AI models (Claude, GPT-4o) through a stateless API key. This means each request is a one-way transaction: your prompt goes out, the response comes back, and nothing from your firm — no documents, no financials, no client names — is ever stored, logged, or retained on any external server. The model answers and forgets. Your data stays yours.

🖥️

Desktop-Resident — Never Statefully in the Cloud

Traditional SaaS AI tools store your conversations, your documents, your history in their database — creating a permanent record of your firm's most sensitive work on someone else's servers. LexDesk is fundamentally different: it runs entirely on each employee's local machine. The only network call is a live, encrypted API request. No storage. No persistent sessions. No cloud database holding your deal flow.

📁

Controlled File Access — Right Documents, Right People

Each employee only accesses the files and data relevant to their role. Analysts working on a specific deal see the documents for that deal. Senior partners see what they need to see. No information sprawl, no accidental exposure. You define the access structure; LexDesk enforces it.

🎛️

Monitor & Adjust in a Moment's Notice

As your firm's needs evolve, LexDesk evolves with it. Add a new tool. Update a workflow. Change the AI prompts behind a template. Push the update and every desktop is running the new version within minutes. You're always in control of what your team can do and how they do it.

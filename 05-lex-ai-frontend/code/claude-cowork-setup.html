The Compliance Gap

AI is in your firm.
The audit trail isn't.

Financial services firms are adopting LLMs faster than their compliance frameworks can keep up. The result is liability — not leverage.

📋

No Immutable Record

Off-the-shelf AI tools offer no WORM-compliant storage. When a regulator asks for the prompt that generated a client communication, you have nothing.

Investment Advice Creep

Analysts prompt Claude for "analysis" and get outputs indistinguishable from a recommendation. Without real-time flagging, it ships to clients unchecked.

Regulatory Exposure

SEC's 2024 AI Staff Bulletin and FINRA guidance place AI-generated communications squarely in scope for supervision. The question is when, not if, you're examined.
